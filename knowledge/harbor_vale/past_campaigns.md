# Harbor & Vale Retention Campaign Results, Last Two Quarters

> Synthetic internal document written for this course project. Harbor & Vale and every
> name, policy and figure below are fictional. These are not results computed from the
> project's dataset.

Owner: Retention Analytics. Uplift is measured against a random holdout group of the same segment.

## Campaign "Welcome Back" (new customers)
- Target: customers in their first two months, flagged by the model.
- Offer: welcome call plus a free-delivery voucher on the second order.
- Result: churn in the treated group was 9 percentage points lower than in the holdout.
- Lesson: the call mattered more than the voucher; customers who only got the voucher by email
  showed almost no uplift.

## Campaign "Wallet Boost" (cashback)
- Target: medium-risk customers paying by UPI or E wallet.
- Offer: INR 150 cashback on the next order above INR 1,000.
- Result: 3 percentage points lower churn than the holdout; cost per retained customer was high.
- Lesson: cashback works for wallet users but is wasted on COD customers, who cannot use it.

## Campaign "Sorry We Missed" (complaints)
- Target: customers with a complaint in the last month.
- Offer: coupon of INR 250 sent the same day as the complaint.
- Result: no improvement over the holdout; churn was slightly higher among customers whose
  complaint was still open when the coupon arrived.
- Lesson: this campaign led to the "resolve before you reward" rule in the playbook.

## Campaign "Mobile Upgrade" (mobile phone buyers)
- Target: customers whose preferred category is Mobile or Mobile Phone.
- Offer: extended warranty on the next phone accessory order.
- Result: 5 percentage points lower churn than the holdout.
- Lesson: the two category spellings responded differently; analyse them separately.
