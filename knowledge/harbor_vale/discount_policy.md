# Harbor & Vale Discount and Retention Budget Policy

> Synthetic internal document written for this course project. Harbor & Vale and every
> name, policy and figure below are fictional.

Owner: Finance and Customer Retention. Amounts are in Indian rupees (INR).

## Monthly retention budget
The retention budget is INR 1,200,000 per month. It is split as follows:
- 50% for high-risk customers (risk score 0.70 and above)
- 30% for medium-risk customers (0.40 to 0.70)
- 20% reserve for complaint recovery, released by the support lead

Unused budget does not roll over to the next month.

## Limits per customer
- Maximum discount per customer per month: INR 500 or 15% of the next order, whichever is lower.
- Cashback offers are capped at INR 300 per customer per month and are paid to the wallet.
- A customer may receive at most one retention offer per 30 days.

## Approval levels
- Up to INR 300: the retention agent decides.
- INR 301 to 500: team lead approval in the CRM.
- Above INR 500: not allowed for retention; escalate to the Head of Customer Experience.

## Offers that need extra care
- Free delivery vouchers are preferred for cash on delivery customers.
- Do not offer discounts in the Grocery category: its churn is already low and margins are thin.
- Offers to customers with a risk score below 0.40 are not funded from this budget.
