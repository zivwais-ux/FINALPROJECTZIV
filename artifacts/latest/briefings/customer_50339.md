## Recommended action
- Contact this customer first by phone call as they are high risk and a new customer flagged by the model. Prioritize customers with complaints and high risk scores [retention_playbook.md > Who we contact first], [retention_playbook.md > Channels].
- Since the customer is new (tenure 0 months), make a welcome call within 3 days of the flag and follow up with an email about delivery and payment experience. Do not offer a discount on this first contact; focus on fixing the experience first [retention_playbook.md > New customers (tenure 0 to 1 months)].
- Resolve the customer's complaint before offering any coupon or discount. Coupons sent while complaints are open may increase churn [retention_playbook.md > Customers who complained].
- For COD customers, use free-delivery vouchers or a small discount printed on the next invoice rather than cashback, and encourage but do not require switching to UPI or card payment [retention_playbook.md > Cash on delivery (COD) customers].

## Offer
- No discount on first contact due to new customer status.
- If still at risk after a week, consider a free-delivery voucher as preferred for COD customers [discount_policy.md > Offers that need extra care].
- Maximum discount per customer per month is INR 500 or 15% of the next order, whichever is lower. Cashback offers capped at INR 300 per month and paid to wallet (not applicable here) [discount_policy.md > Limits per customer].
- Retention agent can approve offers up to INR 300; above that requires team lead approval [discount_policy.md > Approval levels].

## Do not
- Do not send coupons or discounts before complaint resolution [retention_playbook.md > Customers who complained].
- Do not offer discounts on the first contact for new customers [retention_playbook.md > New customers (tenure 0 to 1 months)].
- Do not use cashback offers for COD customers [retention_playbook.md > Cash on delivery (COD) customers].
