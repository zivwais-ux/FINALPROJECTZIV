# Competitor Offers Tracker

> Synthetic internal document written for this course project. Harbor & Vale, CartNest,
> BazaarLane and QuickKart are fictional companies; every offer below is invented.

Owner: Category Management. Updated monthly from public advertising.

## CartNest
- Positioning: low prices on electronics, especially phones and laptops.
- Current offer: 10% off the first phone order for new customers, paid as instant discount.
- Threat to us: high for customers whose preferred category is Mobile or Mobile Phone.

## BazaarLane
- Positioning: fashion and lifestyle, strong mobile app.
- Current offer: free delivery with no minimum order for app users.
- Threat to us: medium for Fashion customers who log in from a phone.

## QuickKart
- Positioning: fast grocery delivery within 30 minutes in tier 1 cities.
- Current offer: cash on delivery with no extra fee.
- Threat to us: low, because our Grocery customers churn least.

## How to use this tracker
Use it to choose a counter-offer that matches what a customer is likely being offered elsewhere,
within the limits of the discount policy. It describes competitors' advertising, not their
results; do not quote competitor figures to customers.
